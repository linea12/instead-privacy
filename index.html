Privacy Policy

For the Android app Instead
Last updated: 12 August 2026

Instead is made by LaArtHeuser. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled. Instead is designed so that your usage data stays on your phone.

What Instead does

Instead helps you use selected apps more intentionally. You choose apps to limit, set optional time budgets and night windows, and set up needs with apps and short actions. When a limit applies, Instead can show a short pause so you can choose what to do next.

Data stored on your device

Instead stores settings and local usage information on your phone, such as:

  • Which apps you limited, and the limits you chose for each one
  • Your needs, assigned apps, and actions
  • Local insights about pauses and app use on this device
  • Design preferences (for example theme and accent color)
  • Whether setup is finished and similar app preferences

This information is kept in the app’s private storage on your device. Instead does not create an account for you and does not upload this data to a LaArtHeuser cloud service.

No account, no cloud from Instead

Instead does not require a login. LaArtHeuser does not operate a cloud backend for Instead that receives your usage history or settings.

Android access Instead needs

To work as described, Instead may ask for Android permissions or settings, including:

  • Usage access — so Instead can notice when a selected app is open and apply the limits you chose
  • Display over other apps — so Instead can come to the front and show a short pause when needed
  • Optional settings such as battery unrestricted or notifications, so the phone is less likely to stop Instead in the background and so you can receive quiet status notices

These capabilities are used only for the features you enable in Instead. They are not used to build an advertising profile for LaArtHeuser.

External apps and websites

From Instead you may open other apps (for example apps you assigned to a need) or website links (for example an optional link in an action, the Play Store during setup, or a support page).

When you leave Instead and open another app or website, that app or site has its own privacy policy and may collect data or use tracking under its own rules. Instead does not control those third parties.

Ko-fi (optional donations)

Instead may include a link to a Ko-fi page so you can optionally tip the developer. Opening that link takes you to Ko-fi’s website. You are then subject to Ko-fi’s own Privacy Policy: https://ko-fi.com/privacy.

Donation support page: https://ko-fi.com/laartheuser.

Children

Instead is not directed at children under 13, and LaArtHeuser does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Instead.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when it does. Continued use of Instead after an update means you accept the revised policy.
